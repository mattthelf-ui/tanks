Haywire: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy covers the Haywire game for Android and the web version at mattthelf-ui.github.io/tanks.

Data we collect

None. Haywire does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no user accounts and no sign-in.

Ads, analytics and third parties

Haywire has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. There are no in-app purchases.

Saved games

Your progress and settings (for example difficulty, best rounds, bot mastery and audio settings) are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Network access

The Android app does not request internet permission and makes no network requests; all game files are bundled in the app. The web version only loads its own game files from GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

Children

Because Haywire collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
